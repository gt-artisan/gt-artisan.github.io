<section class="sh-section" id="research">
  <h2>Areas of focus</h2>
  <p class="sh-note">We begin with the scientific question, then shape the approach and build the AI, software, and computing needed to answer it.</p>
  <ul class="sh-plain">
    <li><strong>AI-driven solutions for science and engineering.</strong> Foundation and domain models, AI agents, and surrogate models, trained and run at HPC scale.</li>
    <li><strong>Cyberinfrastructure and distributed systems.</strong> Gateways, workflows, data movement, and identity, connecting laptops, clouds, and national supercomputers.</li>
    <li><strong>Interpretable AI for scientific discovery.</strong> Methods that show what a scientific model has learned, from protein structure prediction to molecular simulation.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="sh-section" id="projects">
  <p class="sh-eyebrow">Projects</p>
  <h2>What we are building</h2>
  <ul class="sh-projects">
    <li class="sh-wide">
      <span class="sh-pname">Nexus</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">A very large AI supercomputing resource for open science, built and operated by Georgia Tech and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. It gives researchers an integrated environment for moving from interactive development to large-scale workloads.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://nexus.gatech.edu/">nexus.gatech.edu</a><a href="https://docs.nexus.gatech.edu/">Docs</a><a href="https://portal.nexus.gatech.edu/">Portal</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">CyberShuttle</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Connects researchers, applications, data, and workflows across local, cloud, and HPC resources, using familiar tools.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://cybershuttle.org/">cybershuttle.org</a><a href="https://github.com/cyber-shuttle/cybershuttle-ai4science-foundry">AI4Science Foundry</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">AI2Science</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">A research program on what foundation models actually learn about science, and how that structure can be tested and turned into scientific claims.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://ai2science.github.io">ai2science.github.io</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">Custos</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Open-source security for science gateways: federated login through CILogon, secure credential management, groups and sharing, and audit logs.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://airavata.apache.org/custos/">Apache Custos</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">VizFold</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Makes AlphaFold and similar protein-folding models interpretable, with interactive exploration in the browser. Funded under an NSF NAIRR EAGER award.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://vizfold.github.io">vizfold.github.io</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">SciGaP</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Hosted services for science gateways, including user management, workflow execution, and experiment archiving. Built on Apache Airavata.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://scigap.org/">scigap.org</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">SMILES</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Dense lattices of fluorescent dyes that avoid self-quenching, giving programmable, very bright emission.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://js2.smiles.scigap.org/">Project site</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">AMOS Gateway</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Free access to state-of-the-art software for atomic, molecular, and optical science, through a coordinated international platform.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://amos-gateway.org/">amos-gateway.org</a></span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="sh-pname">Plasma VLab</span>
      <span class="sh-pdesc">Virtual laboratory resources for plasma research, with computing access to the Gkeyll code.</span>
      <span class="sh-links"><a href="https://vlab.plasmascience.scigap.org/">Project site</a></span>
    </li>
  </ul>

<section class="sh-section" id="collaboration">
  <p class="sh-eyebrow">Collaboration</p>
  <h2>How we work with science teams</h2>
  <p class="sh-lede">A collaboration starts with the scientific problem, not the technology.</p>
  <p>We partner with research teams at Georgia Tech and beyond to shape the approach, build the methods and software, and run them at the scale the science demands.</p>
  <div class="sh-subnote">
    <p class="sh-plabel">Advanced AI for Science Collaboration (AASC)</p>
    <p>One route in, through Nexus. AASC pairs research teams with AI, HPC, and research engineering experts to move promising AI4Science prototypes toward scalable, reusable, and reproducible capability. It requires an active Nexus access award and does not provide computing resources.</p>
  </div>
</section>
